Controls

          
Auto L-cancel halves aerial attack landing lag, rounded down. Autocancel landings keep their normal recovery.

          
          
Drop in a passive purple opponent to test damage and knockback. Reset clears its damage; knocked-out dummies return automatically.

          
          
Guard: hold the Guard button on the ground for a shield. Tap Guard in the air to spend your air dodge; it returns when you land.

          
Tilt gently to walk. Flick sideways to dash, then keep holding to run. Quick opposite flicks chain tight turns. Hold down to crouch on the ground; swipe down while descending to fast-fall.

          
Tap Jump for a short hop; hold for a full jump. Attack at neutral for a jab on the ground or a neutral aerial. Hold a direction for a tilt or directional aerial; attack shortly after a dash flick or while running for a dash attack. Flick a direction and press Attack together for an uncharged smash. Hold Special to charge Shield Breaker; release to swing. Hold up and press Special for Dolphin Slash. After Dolphin Slash, you must land before acting again. Up does not jump.
